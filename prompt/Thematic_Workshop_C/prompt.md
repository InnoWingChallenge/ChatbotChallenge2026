### Text information: SLA printing poster and safety card

Sources: [IMG_2692](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/All kinds of rooms/IMG_2692.HEIC).

The Thematic Workshop C poster in IMG_2692 is titled "SLA 3D PRINTING". It explains that stereolithography cures photopolymer resin layer by layer with UV light to form a three-dimensional object, and lists fast speed, high resolution and smooth surface finishes as advantages. Its guidance panel names "Form 4 SLA 3D printers" and says they are available under tutor guidance, with help in material selection and machine use to create prototypes for learning purposes. This wording does not specify a printer count. A "Creations Gallery" shows three printed examples, including a dragon-like figure, a dental model and a light-colored fabricated piece.

A separate blue sign reads "Inno Wing Equipment Maintenance Request Form" above a QR code that has not been decoded.

### Text information: induction requirements and printed floor plan

Sources: [IMG_2693](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/All kinds of rooms/IMG_2693.HEIC).

IMG_2693 shows a Thematic Workshop C induction card labeled "INNO Induction", "THEMATIC WORKSHOP 3" and "ADVANCED AND NEW MATERIALS". It describes an advanced 3D printer as micro-fabrication equipment based on Projection Micro Stereolithography, designed to produce high-resolution, highly precise parts for prototyping and short-run production. The card states "TRAINED MEMBERS ONLY", "COURSE REQUIRED", "BOOKING REQUIRED" and "TECHNICIAN'S SUPERVISION". Date-in-service, capacity and social-distancing fields appear blank. The numeric Workshop 3 label is preserved alongside the letter C on the separate room plaque.

Above the induction card in IMG_2693 is a "Fire Escape Route Plan". Its visible labels include Makerspace A, Machine shop, Electronic workbenches, Laser cutting room, Thematic Workshop, Dreamchamber, Staff, 3D Printing Area and Makerspace B. Five gold "EXIT" markers are visible, and a red route is drawn toward the right-hand side of the plan. The marked assembly point is "Top of Suen Yat Sun Steps". This exact wording differs from the "Sun Yat Sen Steps Summit" wording on other fire-action notices; both are preserved as source-specific inscriptions. The plan is printed diagram evidence and does not establish the current names or availability of every room shown.
