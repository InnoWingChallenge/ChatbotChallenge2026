### Location attribution and exact text: maker-space rules

Sources: [maker_space/sign.jpg](../../submission_repo/image_hzq/maker_space/sign.jpg).

The image_hzq maker_space photographs are grouped with the existing Makerspace A record because their window wall, wooden worktables, tall stools and exposed-ceiling layout visually correspond to the Pictures_Hzx Makerspace A overview. None of these four JPEGs visibly names Makerspace A. The sign itself says "MAKER SPACE" without a letter designation, so the assignment is a visual match rather than a new room-name transcription.

The heading on maker_space/sign.jpg reads "DO'S AND DON'TS FOR MAKER SPACE". The four upper instructions, from left to right, read "NO FOOD & DRINKS", "DO NOT REARRANGE TABLE SET", "UNATTENDED ITEMS WILL BE REMOVED" and "DO NOT USE POWER TOOLS & GLUE GUN". The four lower instructions, from left to right, read "USE HEADPHONES FOR MUSIC/VIDEOS/CALLS", "KEEP YOUR BELONGINGS WITH YOU", "KEEP CLEAN AND TIDY" and "USE MAT TO PROTECT THE TABLE". Line breaks are joined, while the photographed wording is retained.

The maker-space rules sign has eight instruction panels arranged in two rows of four. The upper row uses four red prohibition symbols with pictures of food and drink, a table, luggage, and powered tools including a glue gun. The lower row uses four green rings with headphones, a key and phone, a person disposing of rubbish, and a shield. The title band is blue, the body is white with a black border, and each caption sits on a black rectangle. The laminated sheet rests against a light wood surface. These pictograms illustrate rules and are not an equipment inventory. The specific power-tool and glue-gun restriction should retain its sign attribution rather than becoming an inferred prohibition on every tool everywhere in Innovation Wing.
