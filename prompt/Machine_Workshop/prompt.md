### Text information: access, clothing and safety card

Sources: [IMG_2700](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/All kinds of rooms/IMG_2700.HEIC).

IMG_2700 confirms the Machine Workshop entrance instruction "Restricted Area", "WITH TECHNICIANS" and "Please enter the workshop only when technicians are available." The adjacent notice states "CAUTION" and "Safety glasses required". It warns against wearing gloves or loose clothes when using power tools and against sandals, scarves and shorts. A separate clothing illustration contrasts unsuitable dress with close-fitting sleeves, fastened clothing and safety shoes; it calls for protective work clothes, protective footwear and appropriate safety equipment for the job. These photographed notices contain different task-dependent references to gloves and should not be collapsed into a universal instruction to wear gloves around moving machinery.

Safety-awareness entries are General Safety, Electrical Safety and Machinery Safety. Visible hazard labels are "HIGH SPEED MACHINERY" and "HIGH TEMPERATURE". Protection labels are "ENTRY RESTRICTION", "EYE PROTECTION" and "SAFETY SHOES". The card is dated 30/9/2026.

Another sheet in IMG_2700 is headed "Using a fire extinguisher" and "Fire blanket's instructions of use". The extinguisher section states that extinguishers should not be used by people who have not been trained and that the alarm and safe evacuation route should be addressed first. A PASS illustration appears below. The sheet is recorded as entrance signage and is not treated as evidence that fire equipment is currently available or serviced.
