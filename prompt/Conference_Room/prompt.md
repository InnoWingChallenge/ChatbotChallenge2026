### Text information: identity and food restriction

Sources: [IMG_2671](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/G conference room/IMG_2671.HEIC).

IMG_2671 identifies the Conference Room as "IW-G02" on a black-and-wood-grain plaque mounted on glass. A large notice below reads "NO FOOD OR DRINK ALLOWED" and depicts a burger and drink inside a red prohibition symbol. This is the G-numbered Conference Room, distinct from LG14 Dream Chamber.

### Spatial information: long meeting table

Sources: [IMG_2672](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/G conference room/IMG_2672.HEIC).

The Conference Room interior in IMG_2672 has a long white, marble-patterned meeting table with rounded ends and black wheeled office chairs along both sides. A recessed cable-access panel lies near the middle, with small green arrangements farther along the tabletop. Glass partitions run along both sides, including horizontal frosted bands, and a ceiling-mounted projector hangs above the table beneath rectangular light panels. White cabinetry and an awards display occupy the far end. The view does not establish a complete chair count or official booking capacity.

A prominent framed certificate reads "ABU Asia-Pacific Robot Contest 2026 Hong Kong, China", "GRAND PRIX", "Awarded to THE UNIVERSITY OF HONG KONG", and is dated "23rd August, 2026". The dates belong to awards and certificates rather than to the photo capture.
