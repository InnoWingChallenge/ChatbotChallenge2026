## Text information: meeting and conference room rules

Sources: [Source image 56_35](../../image_source/Dream_Chamber/微信图片_20261013104614_56_35.jpg).

The rules sheet supplied for Dream Chamber is headed "The University of Hong Kong", "Faculty of Engineering", "Tam Wing Fan Innovation Wing" and "Rules and Guidelines for Conference Room and Meeting Room". It is marked "Last updated: Nov 25, 2024". Under eligibility, all Innovation Wing members may use or reserve the Meeting Room, whereas only SIG members may use or reserve the Conference Room. The sheet distinguishes these two room categories; the Conference Room restriction should not be generalized to every meeting space.

The photographed rules sheet states that Conference Room and Meeting Room opening hours follow Innovation Wing: Monday to Friday from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm, Saturday from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, and closed on Sundays and public or university holidays. These are the hours printed on the dated sheet, not a live confirmation of current opening hours.

According to the rules sheet photographed for Dream Chamber, Conference Room and Meeting Room use is limited to academic purposes, such as work or discussions related to hands-on projects. Bookings may be made at the Reception counter or by email to innowing@hku.hk on a first-come-first-served basis. Each eligible user may book a maximum of two sessions per day, with each session lasting 60 minutes. The Meeting Room is designated for team meetings or hands-on work activities rather than individual use; the sheet suggests learning commons or library facilities for individual work.

The photographed rules state that Conference Room furniture, equipment and electronic devices belong to Innovation Wing, and users may be charged for loss or damage. Food and beverages are prohibited in both the Conference Room and Meeting Room. Users must keep the rooms clean and tidy, remain responsible for their personal property and never leave belongings unattended. Innovation Wing disclaims responsibility for loss of or damage to personal belongings.

The rules sheet states that Innovation Wing staff may remove belongings or revoke room-use rights when users fail to comply with its regulations. Innovation Wing retains sole and final discretion in interpreting the regulations in a dispute. The earlier rules-sheet photograph left one word unresolved. The clearer Pictures_Hzx image IMG_2653 confirms the final notice: "Rules and guidelines are subject to change without prior notice."

Sources: [Source image 58_35](../../image_source/Dream_Chamber/微信图片_20261013104616_58_35.jpg).

The booking notice supplied for Dream Chamber states "BOOKING IN ADVANCE IS REQUIRED" and "Minimum 2 persons to book the room". It says all bookings must be made in person at the G/F office and requires presentation of a student ID card when booking and entering.
