# Sample Data: Contents

The Workshop 1 corpus and dev set. A fictional institution, the Harbour
Innovation Centre, deliberately not Inno Wing.

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sample_corpus/` | 18 `.txt` pages, 64 to 444 words each |
| `dev_set.json` | 15 questions, three per level, with answers |
| `build_sample_chroma.py` | Builds `sample_chroma/` for lab 1 |
| `build_sample.py` | Regenerates the corpus and dev set |

Why the corpus is shaped the way it is, what each level scores on it,
and how to build `sample_chroma/` are in
`ChatbotChallenge2026_Prep_Checklist.pdf`. This folder holds the data,
not the explanation.
